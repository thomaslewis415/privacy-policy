Privacy Policy

Pour Pop  ·  Last updated March 2026

🛡️ We do not collect any personal data.

Overview

Pour Pop is a single-player puzzle game. We built it to be played, not to harvest your information. No personal data is collected, stored, or shared — period.

What We Don't Collect

  • No name, email address, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No browsing or usage analytics
  • No camera, microphone, or photo library access
  • No data shared with third-party advertisers

Game Progress & Settings

Your level progress, scores, and settings are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all transaction data.

Leaderboard

The optional global leaderboard uses Game Center, which is managed by Apple. Your Game Center display name and score may be visible to other players if you choose to participate. You can opt out at any time through your device's Game Center settings.

Children's Privacy

Pour Pop does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The game is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. We will never introduce data collection without clearly updating this policy first.
